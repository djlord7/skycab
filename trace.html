Step 1: make the screen show true size

The browser doesn't know how big your screen's pixels are, so match it once with something you can measure. Keep the browser zoom at 100% (Ctrl+0) while you do this and while you trace.

  1. Hold a ruler against the screen on the black line below.
  2. Drag the slider (or use the − / + buttons) until the line is exactly 10 cm on your ruler.
  3. Check with a bank card (any credit/debit card is 85.6 × 54 mm): it should fit the box exactly.
